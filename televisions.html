Television Energy Consumption

In the Australian market, televisions are rated using the Energy Rating Label (stars).

  • LED TVs: Typically use 40-100 watts depending on size. Highly efficient.
  • OLED TVs: Use slightly more power due to self-emissive pixels, around 80-150 watts.
  • QLED TVs: Often brighter but consume between 100-200 watts for large 65-inch models.

Pro Tip: Always turn off your TV at the wall. Leaving it on standby consumes roughly 5-10 watts continuously!
